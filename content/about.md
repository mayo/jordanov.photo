I can’t quite remember when my fascination with photography began, but my curiosity about the process started early. My grandpa had a bunch of cameras and always took photos, and I’ve always been fascinated by the craft. I remember watching him changing film in black bags under duvet covers. I remember playing with his cameras pretending to take photos and figuring out how they worked, and the variety of sizes and formats of film and prints that were abound in boxes. I remember getting one of his cameras and taking it on a school trip in middle school.

I really started getting into photography later on, around the time of digital cameras, albeit with a film camera. I had a Kodak DC40 around 1999, but I really got into it with a film Canon Rebel Ti. It was a good camera for me, but I eventually outgrew it, switching to full digital with a Canon 20D. I’ve mostly shot digital since, but I’ve always kept dabbling with film. Some of my most favourite cameras over time: Canon 5D mkII, Sony RX1, Sony 7R m4, Polaroid SX70, and Kiev 80.

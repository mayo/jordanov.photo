Overexposed shot from Ambleside pier.

My first attempt at using PX 680 film in a SX 70 camera with a ND filter. Except I misread the instructions and assumed the filter did full correction and I dind't have to adjust the light/dark camera setting all the way to dark.

While it is completely overexposed, I like how that one building that was in shade sticks out and looks almost like a drawing.

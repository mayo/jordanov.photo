Icicles hanging in front of panoramic window of Horstman Hut at Whistler.

One of my least trips to the top of the hill this season. The snow is being melted right in front of my eyes and  the mountain shuts down in two weeks. This, combined with my schedule over the coming weeks probably means this was my last trip up. No worries though, the bike park is opening soon and the next season is right around the corner. Here's to a better start of next season.
